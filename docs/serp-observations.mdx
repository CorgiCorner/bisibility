---
title: "SERP positions and observations"
description: "Understand organic rankings, local-pack results, and AI Overview observations without confusing them with AI citation tracking."
---

A rank check measures an organic position. The same provider response can also
contain search-result features, recorded as separate observations.

| Result | What it tells you | What it does not tell you |
| - | - | - |
| Organic position | The best matching organic result within the requested depth | An absolute screen position that counts ads and other blocks |
| Local-pack observation | A business result returned inside a Google web-search local pack | Rankings across a Maps geographic grid |
| AI Overview observation | An AI Overview block returned in the checked Google results | Whether an answer engine cites your brand across prompts or conversations |

## Read a completed check

The keyword fixes the market, language, and device. Its organic `position`
does not move just because the provider includes a local pack or AI Overview.
`position: null` means no matching organic result was found within the checked
depth when the check completed successfully. It does not mean a provider failed.

See [Checks](/api/checks) for completion, queued runs, and failure handling.

## Understand observation coverage

DataForSEO and SerpApi adapters can record local-pack and AI Overview data when
their response includes it. Coverage depends on the provider response, requested
scope, and completeness. Missing data is not proof that a feature never appeared
or that a brand is absent from every AI answer.

An AI Overview observation can preserve a block title and provider fragment.
It does not establish a normalized citation, citation share, sentiment score,
or cross-engine AI visibility measurement. Dedicated AI visibility and LLM
citation tracking remain separate from this capability.

Storage support does not imply that every observation field is exposed by every
client. Consult the generated **API Reference** for response fields and
[released client support](/compatibility) for inspected client versions.
