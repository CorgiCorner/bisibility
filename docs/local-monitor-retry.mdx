---
title: "Retry local checks"
description: "Review and retry an eligible failed subset without purchasing the original run again."
---

Open a Local monitor's original run and select the failed points offered for
retry. A retry is a **new paid run**, with its own quote and budget checks.
Selecting points or refreshing the page does not submit it.

## Which points can be retried?

A point is eligible only after the original attempt has a confirmed rejection or
proof that nothing was sent, and its accounting is complete.

Accepted tasks, unknown acceptance, pending accounting, and requests still owned
by collection or recovery are not eligible. Continue collecting or reconciling
the original request instead of submitting a duplicate.

## Review the new request

1. Select the eligible failed points.
2. Review the new quote, selected point count, original point count, and expiry.
3. Confirm while the quote and source evidence are still valid.

Changing the selection or provider connection requires a fresh preview. The
server rechecks your access, monitor revision, original evidence, quote, and
budgets before submission.

## Compare results carefully

The retry contains only the selected targets. Its coverage uses that subset as
the denominator; the original run and its full denominator remain unchanged.
Do not interpret a subset retry as a new measurement of the whole grid.

See [Local providers](/local-search-provider) for source and coverage limits,
or [schedule approval](/local-monitor-recurring) for recurring work.
