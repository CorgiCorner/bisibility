---
title: "Schedule local checks"
description: "Approve a bounded schedule for a Local monitor and review its retained results."
---

Local monitor schedules are available only when recurring Local execution is
enabled on the instance. They require a running worker and a supported provider
connection. Creating a monitor or opening its page does not start a paid check.

## Approve a schedule

1. Open the monitor under **Settings → Business locations**.
2. Choose a calendar and your provider connection.
3. Set the maximum cost per occurrence, total approved cost, maximum number of
   measurements, and approval end date.
4. Preview the scope and current quote, then confirm the schedule.

You need write access to approve or pause a schedule. Project and connection
budgets still apply in addition to the schedule's limits.

The total approval limit counts issued quotes conservatively. It is separate
from settled provider charges: an issued quote can still consume that approval
when a check does not finish. Unknown acceptance or cost is never treated as zero.

## Changes and pauses

Changing the monitored targets, provider credentials, calendar, or pricing
requires a new review. Expired approval does not authorize another occurrence.
Completely elapsed calendar intervals are skipped rather than purchased as a
backlog.

Pausing prevents future submissions. Already accepted work can still finish
collection and accounting. Refreshing the page reads retained results; it does
not purchase another check.

## Review results

Choose the last 7, 28, or 90 days, or all retained history. The interval uses the
original observation time. Narrow a large interval if the page asks you to;
an incomplete result is not presented as complete history.

For setup, see [Local providers](/local-search-provider) and
[worker topologies](/self-hosting/docker#compose-topology-commands). For failed
points, see [Retry local checks](/local-monitor-retry).
