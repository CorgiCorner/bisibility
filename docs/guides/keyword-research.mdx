Keyword Research looks up related phrases, suggestions, and ideas from one seed
through the project's connected DataForSEO account. It does not start rank
checks. The page is project-level; it does not follow the header market
switcher.

## What you need

- A connected DataForSEO account on [Integrations](/integrations).
- Remaining monthly provider budget. Fresh lookups spend that budget. Cached
  repeats are free.

## Run research

1. Open **Keyword Research**.
2. Choose a country and language in the module. Options come from the project
   default and from [markets](/guides/manage-markets) you already created. A
   city or region is translated to its country while keeping the language.
3. Enter one seed phrase. Choose **Auto**, **Related**, **Suggestions**, or
   **Ideas**, and how many unique results you want (100, 300, or 500).
4. Review the cost on **Research**. The button shows `~$X` before a paid
   lookup, or **Research free, cached** when the same result is already stored.
5. Run the lookup. Rows show Keyword, Volume, Trend, Keyword difficulty (KD),
   Cost per click (CPC), Intent, and Source.

Clickstream-refined volumes cost more. If the monthly provider budget is
exhausted, fresh lookups are disabled until it resets; cached recent searches
remain available.

An estimate-only dry run is available from the
[Keyword research API](/api/keyword-research) if you want the cost without
rows.

## What to do with the result

Select phrases and **Save for later**, or open the add-keyword flow to start
tracking. Saved phrases also appear on
[Saved keywords](/guides/saved-keywords). Research rows are not tracked until
you add them as keywords. Already-tracked phrases are marked **Tracked**.

API request shapes stay on [Keyword research](/api/keyword-research).
