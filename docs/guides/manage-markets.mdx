---
title: "Manage markets"
description: "Create, select, pause, and archive project markets before importing keywords."
---

A market is a location and language pair. Tracked keywords add a device, so one
phrase can have separate desktop and mobile targets in the same market. Create
markets before you import keywords; imports never create a missing market.

Country and language lists live in the
[Markets and languages](/markets) reference.

## Create a market

1. Open **Markets**.
2. Select **New market**.
3. Choose a **Country**, then a **Language**. Language stays disabled until the
   country is set. Suggested languages appear first; the full list is searchable.
4. Leave **Location** on the whole country, or search for a city or region.
5. Optionally add a **Custom name**.
6. Choose **Devices**: Desktop, Mobile, or Both.
7. Choose how the market gets keywords: **Copy from market**, **Paste
   keywords**, or **Start empty**. Copy and paste also need a **Schedule** (or
   **Manual**).
8. Select **Create market**. Creating the market spends nothing.

Empty projects show **Track your first market**. You can also open the same
sheet from the header switcher with **Add market**.

## Select a market

Use the header switcher next to the page title. The list includes **All
markets**, then each market. Search **Find market...** appears when the project
has more than six markets.

Selecting a market scopes **Rank Tracker**. Other modules stay at the project
level and do not follow this switcher. Opening a market name from the Markets
table goes to that market's Rank Tracker.

Opening **Import CSV** from a market preselects it as the **Market for rows
without a location**.

## Add keywords to a market

From **Markets**, use the row menu **Add keywords**. The add drawer opens with
that market selected. **Markets** and **Devices** are required there. A paused
market still accepts keywords; the drawer says **Add paused keywords**, and
rank checks wait until you resume the market.

Keywords attach by the market's location, not by a separate market field on
each phrase.

## Pause, resume, and archive

The Markets page has **Active and paused** and **Archived** tabs. Active and
paused markets appear in separate tables.

- Use the status switch to **Pause** or **Resume** a market. Rank checks run
  only while the market is active.
- **Archive market** stops tracking for its keywords. Existing rank history
  stays readable. It does not delete keywords.
- **Restore** from **Archived** brings the market back as active and resumes
  its keywords on their schedules.

Paused and archived markets are not runnable. The app reports **Market not
active** when a check is stopped for that reason.

## Import behavior

Create the project's markets first, then select **Import CSV**. Continue stays
disabled until at least one market exists. Choose the **Market for rows
without a location** before uploading. To import into several existing
markets, choose **Use markets from the file** and provide `country` with the
matching `language`, or an exact `location_key`. Unknown locations are
rejected. See [Add your first keywords](/guides/choose-first-keywords).
